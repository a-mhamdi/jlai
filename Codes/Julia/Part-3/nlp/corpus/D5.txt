The heart pumps oxygenated blood through the circulatory system via a network of arteries, veins, and capillaries. An electrocardiogram records the heart's electrical activity, showing the P wave, QRS complex, and T wave that correspond to atrial and ventricular contraction. Physicians use this pattern to diagnose arrhythmias, ischemia, and other cardiac conditions.

Blood pressure reflects the force exerted on vessel walls as the heart contracts and relaxes, and chronically elevated pressure can damage arteries over time. Risk factors such as high cholesterol, smoking, and diabetes contribute to the buildup of plaque, which narrows blood vessels and restricts blood flow. Treatment options range from lifestyle changes and medication to surgical procedures like angioplasty or bypass grafting when blockages become severe.
