Structural engineers analyze how loads such as gravity, wind, and seismic forces travel through a building's beams, columns, and foundation. Steel and reinforced concrete are common materials because they resist both tension and compression. Finite element analysis allows engineers to simulate stress distribution across a structure before construction begins, helping prevent failure under extreme conditions.

Bridges and high-rise buildings require additional consideration of dynamic loads, including vibration from traffic or resonance during an earthquake. Engineers select material thickness and joint design to keep deflection within safe limits while minimizing weight and cost. Regular inspection and maintenance are essential, since fatigue and corrosion can weaken a structure's load-bearing capacity over time.
